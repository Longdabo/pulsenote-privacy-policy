PulseNote Support

Get help

Email longdabo@gmail.com for help with recording, transcription, voice pack downloads, or exports. Include your device model, OS version, and PulseNote version. Keep private recordings and transcripts out of your email.

You can record immediately. To transcribe, connect to the internet and download a voice pack under Settings → Offline Voice Packs. Once the pack is installed, transcription runs offline.

PulseNote Privacy Policy

声记技术支持

如需录音、转写、语音包下载或导出方面的帮助，请联系 longdabo@gmail.com。请注明设备型号、系统版本和声记版本，并避免在邮件中附上私人录音或转写内容。

打开应用即可录音。转写前请联网，在「设置 → 离线语音包」下载所需语音包。安装完成后即可离线转写。
